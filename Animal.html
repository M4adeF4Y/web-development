Animal Information

Snow Leopard

TYPE: Big Cat

LIVES IN: Mountain ranges of Central Asia

DIET: Deer, goats, and wild sheep

COOL FACT: Can leap up to 15 metres in one jump!

Bengal Tiger

TYPE: Big Cat

LIVES IN: Forests and grasslands of India

DIET: Deer, wild boar, and other mammals

COOL FACT: Can run up to 65 km/h!

Giant Panda

TYPE: Bear

LIVES IN: Bamboo forests of China

DIET: Bamboo shoots and leaves

COOL FACT: Have a "pseudo-thumb" to help them grip bamboo!

African Elephant

TYPE: Mammal

LIVES IN: Savannas, forests, and deserts of Africa

DIET: Grass, leaves, and fruits

COOL FACT: Can communicate using infrasound that travels long distances!

Kangaroo

TYPE: Marsupial

LIVES IN: Grasslands and forests of Australia

DIET: Grass, leaves, and fruits

COOL FACT: Can hop at speeds of up to 70 km/h!

Polar Bear

TYPE: Mammal

LIVES IN: Arctic regions

DIET: Seal, fish, and other marine mammals

COOL FACT: Have a thick layer of blubber to keep them warm!
