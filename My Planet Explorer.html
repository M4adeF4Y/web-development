Featured Planets

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life.

Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and the second-smallest planet in the Solar System.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest in the Solar System.

Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and is known for its prominent ring system.

Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is known for its extreme surface temperatures and thick atmosphere.

Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and is known for its rapid orbit and extreme temperature variations.

Neptune

Neptune is the eighth planet from the Sun and is known for its deep blue color and strong winds.

Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and is known for its unique tilt and icy composition.

Featured Stars

Sun

The Sun is the star at the center of the Solar System and is the primary source of energy for life on Earth.

Sirius

Sirius is the brightest star in the night sky and is part of the constellation Canis Major.

Betelgeuse

Betelgeuse is a red supergiant star in the constellation Orion and is one of the largest stars visible to the naked eye.

Rigel

Rigel is a blue supergiant star in the constellation Orion and is one of the most luminous stars known.
